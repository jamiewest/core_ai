## 0.1.0

* Initial release: Pigeon bindings for Apple's Translation framework.
* Language resolution and availability checks on iOS 18+ / macOS 15+.
* Installed-language translation sessions on iOS 26+ / macOS 26+, including
  single translations, ordered batches, streaming batches and cancellation.
* Quality/speed strategies and protected text segments on iOS 26.4+ /
  macOS 26.4+.
* Typed errors, explicit session disposal, leak queries and injectable bindings.
* Material example with language selection, pack checks, strategy selection,
  single-text translation and streaming per-line translation.
* Unit, widget and real-framework integration tests, plus a device test driver.
